# Playground: EU Fashion Omnichannel

**Segment:** Mid-market fashion retailer, €2–4B revenue, 8 EU countries, 600 stores.

**Economics:** Gross margin 45–52%, inventory turns 4–6x/year, online mix 35–40%, peak season (Oct–Dec) drives 45% of annual revenue.

**Regulatory:** GDPR (customer data), PSD2 SCA (payments), local payment methods (Klarna, Postepay, Giropay).

**Dominant problem:** Inventory fragmentation across 8 regional systems causes 12% phantom stock on click-&-collect, costing €8M/year in lost margin + customer churn to Zalando/ASOS.
