product: Meridian click-&-collect (product detail page)
feature: AI availability assistant — per-store collectability confidence signal
target-user: Click-&-collect shoppers reserving online to avoid a wasted pickup trip
behaviour: Before committing to a reservation, the shopper sees a High / Medium / Low confidence indicator for their nearest store, derived from SAP inventory sync + store-level adjustment signals
success: Click-&-collect cancellation-at-pickup rate drops from 7% to ≤4% within 90 days of launch
out-of-scope: Real-time staff confirmation, home-delivery availability, product recommendations, checkout flow changes
